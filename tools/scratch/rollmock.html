ROUNDNESS BY THE REAL CAR (0 sharp / boxy - 1 round)

Round: keeps more spin at each hit, rolls further. Sharp or boxy: keeps less, and an edge may DIG IN (the spin all but stops there, often on a side or the roof). Open cars catch a wheel or wing. A low smooth wedge slides further on its roof.
